# **Frequently Asked Questions**

### **What are NLP and computer vision development services?**

NLP and computer vision development services create AI-powered software capable of processing language and visual data, including text, speech, images and video, and integrating the resulting outputs into applications and business workflows.

### **What is natural language processing?**

Natural language processing is an area of AI that enables software to analyse and work with human language, including text classification, entity extraction, sentiment analysis and semantic search.

### **What is computer vision?**

Computer vision is an area of AI that enables software to analyse images and video to identify objects, classifications, patterns or other visual information.

### **What is the difference between NLP and computer vision?**

NLP primarily processes language such as text and speech, while computer vision processes visual information such as images and video.

### **Can NLP and computer vision work together?**

Yes. They can be combined in multimodal systems such as document intelligence, where computer vision or OCR extracts visual information and NLP interprets the resulting text.

### **What is document AI?**

Document AI combines technologies such as image processing, OCR and NLP to extract and interpret structured information from documents.

### **What is the difference between computer vision and image processing?**

Image processing mainly transforms or prepares visual data, while computer vision aims to interpret the content or meaning of images and video.

### **Can computer vision analyse video?**

Yes. Computer vision can be applied to video frames for tasks such as object detection, tracking, classification and event analysis.

### **What is speech recognition?**

Speech recognition converts spoken audio into text or another machine-readable representation that can be used by software or further processed with NLP.

### **Can NLP and computer vision models integrate with existing software?**

Yes. AI capabilities can often be connected with existing web, mobile and business applications through APIs, backend services or dedicated integration layers.

### **Do NLP and computer vision projects require custom model training?**

Not always. Depending on the use case, a project may use an existing model, fine-tune an existing model or train a custom model.

### **Can NLP and computer vision work in real time?**

Some NLP and computer vision applications can provide real-time or near-real-time processing where the model, infrastructure and software architecture support it.

### **How are NLP and computer vision models evaluated?**

Evaluation depends on the task and can include metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, F1 score, retrieval relevance, detection performance or other application-specific measures.

### **How much does NLP and computer vision development cost?**

Cost depends on data preparation, model complexity, training requirements, integrations, real-time processing, deployment infrastructure and ongoing model-management requirements.

### **How long does NLP and computer vision development take?**

The timeline depends on data readiness, model requirements, training or fine-tuning, integration, testing and production deployment requirements.
