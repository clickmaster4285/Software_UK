## **Native vs Cross-Platform Mobile App Development**

Choosing between native and cross-platform development is one of the most important decisions in a mobile app project.

| Approach | Best For | Benefit |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| **Native App Development** | iOS-only or Android-only apps with advanced platform needs | Strong performance and deeper platform control |
| **Cross-Platform Development** | Apps needed on both iOS and Android | Faster delivery and easier maintenance |
| **MVP App Development** | Testing an app idea before full investment | Launch faster with core features |
| **Progressive Web App** | App-like browser experience without app store dependency | Useful for some lightweight use cases |

If your app needs advanced device features, native development may be better. If you want to launch on both iOS and Android with a practical budget, cross-platform development may be the better option.
