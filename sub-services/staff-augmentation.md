# **SECTION 02 — DEFINITION**

## **What Is IT Staff Augmentation?**

IT staff augmentation is a resourcing model where external technical specialists join an organisation's existing team to provide additional skills or development capacity.

Unlike outsourcing an entire software project or establishing a complete dedicated development team, staff augmentation keeps the client's existing team and delivery structure at the centre of the engagement.

A typical model can look like:

**Existing Development Team**

* **Additional Technical Specialists**  
   → **Expanded Engineering Capacity**  
   → **Development & Collaboration**  
   → **Software Delivery**

Staff augmentation can be useful where a business already has development leadership and processes but needs additional technical resources.

# **SECTION 08 — ENGAGEMENT MODEL**

## **Staff Augmentation vs Dedicated Development Teams**

These two outsourcing models address different requirements.

| IT Staff Augmentation | Dedicated Development Team |
| ----- | ----- |
| Adds specialists to your existing team | Provides a dedicated external team |
| Existing client team remains central | External team acts as a delivery unit |
| Useful for capacity or skill gaps | Useful for ongoing broader development |
| Role-focused | Team-focused |
| Client typically retains delivery structure | Team works around an agreed roadmap |
| Can add specific capabilities | Can combine several complementary roles |

Choose staff augmentation where you primarily need **additional people inside your current delivery structure**.

For a complete external engineering unit, explore our [**Dedicated Development Team Services**](https://clickmasterssoftwaredevelopmentcompany.co.uk/support-and-outsourcing/dedicated-development-teams).

## **Staff Augmentation vs Project Outsourcing**

Staff augmentation and project outsourcing also differ.

| Staff Augmentation | Project Outsourcing |
| ----- | ----- |
| Adds people to an existing team | Outsources a defined project |
| Client retains greater day-to-day delivery control | Provider manages agreed project delivery |
| Works inside existing processes | Uses project-specific delivery structure |
| Suitable for capacity gaps | Suitable for defined project outcomes |

The right approach depends on whether the business needs **resources** or a **complete delivery responsibility**.

External specialists may require access to existing development environments and software systems.

The engagement can therefore require planning around:

### **Testing Requirements**

Augmented resources should follow the testing and review practices relevant to the client software.

### **Access & Security**

Technical access should reflect the responsibilities of the role and the sensitivity of the systems or data involved.

### **Handover**

Where an engagement ends, relevant code, documentation and development context should remain within the client's normal software-delivery workflow.

# **SECTION 14 — FAQ**

## **Frequently Asked Questions About IT Staff Augmentation**

### **What is IT staff augmentation?**

IT staff augmentation is a resourcing model where external technical specialists join an existing internal team to provide additional development capacity or technical skills.

### **What are staff augmentation services?**

Staff augmentation services provide additional technical resources that work within a client's existing development structure rather than outsourcing the complete project or product team.

### **How does software development staff augmentation work?**

The required technical role is identified, the specialist is onboarded onto the relevant software and development environment, and the resource works alongside the existing team on assigned engineering responsibilities.

### **When should a business use staff augmentation?**

Staff augmentation can be useful when an existing development team needs additional capacity, specialist technical skills or temporary resources for a particular workload.

### **What roles can be provided through staff augmentation?**

Roles depend on the project requirements and can include frontend, backend, full-stack, mobile, QA and DevOps capabilities where appropriate.

### **What is the difference between staff augmentation and a dedicated development team?**

Staff augmentation adds individual specialists to an existing team, while a dedicated development team provides a broader external team working around an ongoing software or product requirement.

### **Is staff augmentation the same as software outsourcing?**

Staff augmentation is a form of outsourcing, but the external specialists generally integrate into the client's existing development team rather than taking responsibility for an entire outsourced project.

### **Can augmented developers work on existing software?**

Yes. Where appropriate, external specialists can be onboarded onto an existing application, codebase and development workflow.

### **Can staff augmentation be used for short-term projects?**

It can support short-term capacity requirements where additional technical resources are needed for a defined period or workload.

### **Can staff augmentation support long-term development?**

Yes. It can also support longer-running development requirements where an organisation requires sustained additional capacity.

### **Can we add more than one technical specialist?**

The number and type of resources should depend on the workload, team structure and technical requirements.

### **How much do IT staff augmentation services cost?**

Cost depends on the technical roles, number of specialists, required skills, engagement duration and responsibilities assigned to the augmented resources.

### **How long does staff augmentation last?**

The engagement can last for the period required to address the development capacity or skill requirement and can be reviewed as workload changes.
