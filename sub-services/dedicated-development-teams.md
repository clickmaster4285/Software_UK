# **SECTION 02 — DEFINITION**

## **What Is a Dedicated Development Team?**

A dedicated development team is an external software team assigned to work continuously on a client's application, product or development roadmap.

Instead of commissioning each development requirement as a separate project, businesses can establish an ongoing team around their software priorities.

A typical model can look like:

**Business / Product Requirements**  
 → **Dedicated Development Team**  
 → **Planning & Development**  
 → **Testing & Integration**  
 → **Software Delivery**  
 → **Ongoing Iteration**

The team structure depends on the technical requirements, existing internal resources and type of software being developed.

# **SECTION 08 — ENGAGEMENT MODEL**

## **Dedicated Development Team vs Project-Based Development**

These approaches solve different requirements.

| Dedicated Development Team | Project-Based Development |
| ----- | ----- |
| Ongoing team engagement | Defined project scope |
| Suitable for evolving requirements | Suitable for clearer fixed deliverables |
| Continuous development | Project-focused delivery |
| Roadmap can evolve | Scope agreed around project objectives |
| Team builds long-term product context | Team focuses on defined project outcome |
| Useful for ongoing software products | Useful for standalone builds |

A dedicated team can be more appropriate where the software roadmap is expected to evolve over time.

## **Dedicated Development Team vs Staff Augmentation**

These models are related but should not be treated as identical.

| Dedicated Development Team | IT Staff Augmentation |
| ----- | ----- |
| External team unit | Additional individual specialists |
| Team-based engagement | Role-based engagement |
| Can support broader delivery ownership | Extends client's existing team |
| Suitable for ongoing product development | Suitable for capacity or skill gaps |
| Team works around shared roadmap | Specialists work within client structure |
| Multiple complementary roles may be involved | Often centred around specific roles |

For businesses primarily needing additional specialists to join an existing team, explore our [**IT Staff Augmentation Services**](https://clickmasterssoftwaredevelopmentcompany.co.uk/support-and-outsourcing/staff-augmentation).

# **SECTION 14 — FAQ**

## **Frequently Asked Questions About Dedicated Development Teams**

### **What is a dedicated development team?**

A dedicated development team is an external software-development team assigned to work continuously on a client's application, software product or development roadmap.

### **What does a dedicated development team do?**

A dedicated team can support ongoing software development, new features, frontend and backend development, integrations, testing, application improvements and other agreed engineering requirements.

### **When should I hire a dedicated development team?**

A dedicated development team can be suitable when a business needs ongoing software-development capacity, has an evolving product roadmap or requires a stable external engineering team for longer-term delivery.

### **What is the difference between a dedicated team and staff augmentation?**

A dedicated development team provides a team-based external delivery unit, while staff augmentation generally adds individual specialists to an existing internal team.

### **Can a dedicated development team work with our internal developers?**

Yes. Responsibilities can be divided between internal and external teams according to application areas, technical specialties or delivery requirements.

### **Can a dedicated team work on existing software?**

Yes. Where appropriate, a team can assess and onboard onto an existing application, codebase and development environment before continuing development.

### **Can a dedicated team develop new features?**

Yes. Feature development can form part of an ongoing dedicated-team engagement according to the agreed product roadmap and development priorities.

### **Can a dedicated team support software modernisation?**

Yes. A dedicated team can support gradual modernisation, refactoring, integration work and other improvements to existing software.

### **What roles can be included in a dedicated development team?**

The required roles depend on the project and can include frontend, backend, full-stack, mobile, QA and DevOps capabilities where appropriate.

### **Is a dedicated team the same as software outsourcing?**

A dedicated development team is one form of software outsourcing. It provides ongoing external development capacity around a particular product, application or development programme.

### **Can the size of a dedicated team change?**

Team composition can be reassessed where workload, technical priorities or software requirements change.

### **How much does a dedicated development team cost?**

Cost depends on team size, technical roles, software complexity, required capabilities, delivery responsibilities and engagement duration.

### **How long can we use a dedicated development team?**

The engagement can continue according to the product roadmap and development requirements. It is generally designed for ongoing or longer-term software delivery rather than a single isolated task.
