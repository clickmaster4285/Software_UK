## **What Is Predictive Analytics?**

Predictive analytics uses historical and current data with statistical techniques and machine learning models to estimate what is likely to happen in the future.

A predictive analytics workflow can connect:

**Business Data**

→ **Data Preparation**

→ **Predictive Model**

→ **Forecast / Probability / Risk Score**

→ **Business Application**

→ **Decision or Action**

Instead of only analysing what has already happened, predictive analytics helps organisations estimate future demand, customer behaviour, operational requirements, risks and other measurable outcomes.

# **Predictive Analytics vs Business Intelligence**

Predictive analytics and business intelligence support different types of decision-making.

| Business Intelligence | Predictive Analytics |
| ----- | ----- |
| Focuses mainly on historical and current data | Focuses on likely future outcomes |
| Reports what has happened | Estimates what may happen |
| Uses reports and dashboards | Uses predictive models and forecasts |
| Supports descriptive analysis | Supports predictive decision-making |

The two approaches can work together within the same data environment.

# **Predictive Analytics vs Forecasting**

Forecasting is one application of predictive analytics.

| Forecasting | Predictive Analytics |
| ----- | ----- |
| Often estimates future numerical values | Covers a broader range of prediction problems |
| Common in demand and capacity planning | Can predict churn, risk, behaviour and other outcomes |
| Frequently time-based | Can use many different data structures |
| Produces future estimates | Can produce forecasts, scores, probabilities or classifications |

Predictive analytics therefore includes forecasting but is not limited to it.

# **Frequently Asked Questions**

### **What are predictive analytics services?**

Predictive analytics services use historical, operational or real-time data together with statistical methods and machine learning to develop models that estimate future outcomes, trends, probabilities or risks.

### **What does predictive analytics do?**

Predictive analytics identifies patterns in existing data and uses those patterns to estimate likely future events or outcomes such as demand, churn, risk or customer behaviour.

### **What is predictive analytics development?**

Predictive analytics development includes preparing data, developing predictive models, evaluating their performance, integrating predictions with software and deploying models into production environments.

### **What is a predictive model?**

A predictive model is a statistical or machine learning model trained on data to estimate an unknown or future outcome.

### **What data is needed for predictive analytics?**

The required data depends on the prediction target. Useful data generally needs to be relevant, sufficiently complete and representative of the patterns the model is expected to learn.

### **Can predictive analytics use real-time data?**

Yes. Predictive systems can use real-time or near-real-time data where the business requirement and technical architecture support it.

### **Can predictive analytics integrate with existing software?**

Yes. Predictive models can be connected with existing applications, CRMs, ERPs, databases and other business systems through APIs or supporting services.

### **What is demand forecasting?**

Demand forecasting uses historical and contextual data to estimate future demand for products, services or operational capacity.

### **Can predictive analytics predict customer churn?**

Yes, where appropriate historical and behavioural data is available, predictive models can estimate the likelihood that customers may stop using a product or service.

### **What is the difference between predictive analytics and machine learning?**

Predictive analytics focuses on estimating future outcomes. Machine learning is one of the technical approaches that can be used to develop predictive models.

### **What is the difference between predictive analytics and business intelligence?**

Business intelligence generally focuses on reporting and understanding historical or current information, while predictive analytics estimates likely future outcomes.

### **How are predictive models evaluated?**

Predictive models can be evaluated using metrics such as accuracy, precision, recall, F1 score or forecast error, depending on the prediction problem.

### **Why do predictive models need monitoring?**

Model performance can change as production data and business conditions change. Monitoring helps identify performance degradation, data drift and operational issues.

### **How much does predictive analytics development cost?**

Cost depends on data readiness, model complexity, software integration, infrastructure, deployment, monitoring and the wider development scope.

### **How long does predictive analytics development take?**

The timeline depends on data availability, prediction complexity, experimentation, model validation, software integration and deployment requirements.
