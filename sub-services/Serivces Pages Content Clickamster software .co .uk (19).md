# **WooCommerce vs Shopify**

Businesses choosing an ecommerce platform frequently compare WooCommerce with Shopify.

| WooCommerce | Shopify |
| ----- | ----- |
| WordPress-based | Hosted ecommerce platform |

# **Frequently Asked Questions**

### **What are WooCommerce development services?**

WooCommerce development services can include custom store development, theme development, plugin development, checkout customisation, APIs, integrations, migration, performance optimisation, testing and ongoing maintenance.

### **What does a WooCommerce developer do?**

A WooCommerce developer builds and customises WordPress-based ecommerce stores, including themes, plugins, functionality, checkout, APIs and integrations.

### **Is WooCommerce part of WordPress?**

Yes. WooCommerce is an ecommerce platform designed to work within the WordPress ecosystem.

### **Do you provide custom WooCommerce development?**

Yes. Custom development can include store functionality, themes, plugins, checkout requirements, integrations and business-specific workflows.

### **Can you develop custom WooCommerce plugins?**

Custom plugins can be developed where existing extensions do not adequately support the required business functionality.

### **Can WooCommerce integrate with an ERP?**

Yes, where suitable APIs or integration methods are available. WooCommerce can exchange products, stock, customers, orders and other required data with ERP systems.

### **Can WooCommerce integrate with a CRM?**

Yes. WooCommerce can connect with CRM platforms to exchange relevant customer, order and sales information where appropriate integration methods are available.

### **Can an existing ecommerce website be migrated to WooCommerce?**

Yes. Existing stores can be assessed for migration of products, categories, customers, orders, content, URLs and other required information.

### **Will a WooCommerce migration affect SEO?**

Migration can affect organic visibility if URLs, redirects, metadata, internal links, canonicals and other SEO elements are not handled correctly. SEO requirements should therefore be incorporated into migration planning.

### **Is WooCommerce suitable for B2B ecommerce?**

WooCommerce can support B2B requirements such as business accounts, wholesale pricing, customer-specific pricing, bulk ordering and ERP integration through suitable configuration and development.

### **How much does WooCommerce development cost?**

Cost depends on store complexity, theme development, custom plugins, integrations, migration, product requirements, checkout, testing and ongoing support. Discovery is normally required before an accurate estimate can be prepared.

### **How long does WooCommerce development take?**

The timeline depends on scope, custom functionality, products, integrations, migration, design, testing and stakeholder approvals.

### **Do you provide WooCommerce maintenance and support?**

Yes. Ongoing work can include bug fixes, WordPress and WooCommerce updates, plugin compatibility, new functionality, integration changes, security improvements and performance optimisation.
